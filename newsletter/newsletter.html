NEWSLETTER POLICY

TownTalk is a Nigerian-based digital publishing and community platform where registered users and blog owners can create accounts, publish articles, share information, comment on content and participate in discussions across different sections of the platform. This Newsletter Policy explains how TownTalk manages email newsletters and other electronic communications sent to subscribers.

Purpose of the Newsletter

The TownTalk newsletter is intended to keep subscribers informed about relevant content and activities on the platform. Depending on the subscriber's preferences, newsletters may include:
New articles and featured stories;
Popular or trending content;
Updates from selected sections of TownTalk;
Platform announcements;
Community news;
Editorial highlights;
New features or services;
Events or opportunities relating to TownTalk;
Promotional or commercial information, where applicable; and
Other information that may reasonably be of interest to subscribers.

Subscription

Users may subscribe to the TownTalk newsletter by providing their email address through an approved TownTalk subscription form or account-setting. Where consent is required, TownTalk will obtain the subscriber's consent before sending marketing or promotional communications. A person may have a TownTalk account without necessarily subscribing to the marketing newsletter.

Email Information We Collect

When you subscribe to the newsletter, TOWNTALK may collect:
Your name;
Email address;
Newsletter preferences;
Subscription date;
Subscription status;
Records of consent or subscription;
Information about whether an email was delivered, opened or interacted with, where supported by the email service provider; and Other information reasonably necessary to administer the newsletter.
The collection and use of personal data is subject to the TownTalk Privacy Policy.

How We Use Newsletter Information

We use newsletter information to:
Deliver newsletters and communications you have requested;
Manage subscriptions;
Personalise content where appropriate; Understand which content is useful to our subscribers;
Improve our newsletters and communications;
Prevent misuse of our mailing systems;
Maintain records of consent where required;
and Comply with applicable legal obligations.
We will not use your email address for unrelated purposes without an appropriate lawful basis.

Marketing Communications

Where TownTalk sends promotional or marketing communications, we will provide an appropriate mechanism for recipients to opt out. You may unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time. Unsubscribing from marketing emails does not necessarily stop essential service-related communications connected with your TownTalk account. For example, we may still send account verification messages, password-reset messages, security notifications or other necessary transactional communications.

Unsubscribing

Every marketing newsletter should contain an accessible unsubscribe mechanism. You may unsubscribe by:
Clicking the "Unsubscribe" link included in the newsletter;
Changing your communication preferences in your TownTalk account, where available; or Contacting TownTalk through PRIVACY/CONTACT EMAIL.
Once an unsubscribe request is received, TownTalk will take reasonable steps to stop sending the applicable marketing communications within a reasonable period.

Third-Party Email Service Providers

TownTalk may use reputable third-party email service providers to send newsletters. These providers may process subscriber information on TownTalk's behalf for purposes such as:
Sending emails;
Managing subscriber lists;
Delivering communications;
Monitoring delivery;
Detecting spam and abuse; and
Providing newsletter performance statistics.
Where third-party service providers process personal data on behalf of TownTalk, TownTal will take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate privacy and security safeguards are in place.

Newsletter Analytics

TownTalk or its email service provider may use technical information to determine whether newsletters were successfully delivered or interacted with. This information may help us understand subscriber engagement and improve the newsletter. Where applicable, subscribers may be able to manage certain tracking technologies through their email application or device settings.

Data Security

TownTalk takes reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect newsletter subscriber information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss or destruction. However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

TownTalk will retain newsletter subscription information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including maintaining appropriate records of subscriptions and unsubscribe requests. Where a person unsubscribes, TownTalk may retain limited information necessary to ensure that the person is not inadvertently re-added to the marketing mailing list.

Changes to This Policy

TownTalk may update this Newsletter Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology, legal requirements or business practices. The latest version will be published on the TownTalk website with the applicable "Last Updated" date.
