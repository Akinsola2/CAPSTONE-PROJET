ACCESSIBILITY STATEMENT

Towntalk is committed to making our website accessible and easy to use for as many people as possible, including people with disabilities. We want everyone to be able to access our stories, information, and website features with dignity and ease.

Our Accessibility Commitment

Towntalk is working toward making our website conform with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA, an internationally recognised standard for web accessibility. Accessibility is an ongoing process, and we will continue to review and improve the website as our content, technology, and services develop.

Accessibility Features

Where applicable, Town Talk aims to provide an accessible experience through:

  • Clear and logical page structure and headings.
  • Keyboard-accessible navigation and controls.
  • Visible keyboard focus indicators.
  • Alternative text for meaningful images.
  • Sufficient colour contrast between text and backgrounds.
  • Accessible forms and form controls.
  • Descriptive links and buttons.
  • Captions or text alternatives for relevant multimedia.
  • Compatibility with assistive technologies such as screen readers.
  • Responsive layouts across different screen sizes and devices.
  • Text that can be resized without loss of content or functionality.

Third-Party Content

Some content or services displayed on Towntalk may be provided by third parties, including embedded videos, social-media content, advertisements, maps, or other external services. While we seek to use accessible services where possible, we may not have complete control over the accessibility of third-party content.

Known Accessibility Limitations

We are continually reviewing Towntalk for potential accessibility barriers. If we identify areas that do not fully meet our accessibility goals, we will work to improve them.

Feedback and Assistance

We welcome feedback about the accessibility of Towntalk. If you experience difficulty accessing any part of our website, please contact us and, where possible, provide:

  • The webpage or content you were trying to access.
  • A description of the accessibility problem.
  • The device and browser you were using, if known.
  • Any assistance or alternative format you require.

Alternative Access

If you are unable to access particular information or content because of an accessibility barrier, please contact us. Where reasonably possible, we will seek to provide the information or assistance in an accessible alternative format.

Ongoing Improvements

Accessibility is an ongoing commitment at Towntalk. We will continue to review our website, content, and development practices and make improvements to provide a more inclusive experience for our users.
