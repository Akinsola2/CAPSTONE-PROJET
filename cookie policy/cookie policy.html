COOKIE POLICY

What Are Cookies?

Towntalk uses cookies and similar technologies on our website to help the website function properly, remember preferences, understand how visitors use our website, and improve your experience.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They allow a website to recognise your device and remember certain information about your visit. We may also use similar technologies, such as pixels, tags, or local storage, where applicable.

Why We Use Cookies

Towntalk may use cookies for purposes such as:

  • Keeping the website secure and functioning properly.
  • Remembering your preferences and settings.
  • Understanding how visitors use and navigate the website.
  • Measuring website and content performance.
  • Improving the website, its content, and the overall user experience.

Types of Cookies

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to operate properly. They may support security, navigation, session management, and other essential features.

Functional or Preference Cookies

These cookies allow the website to remember choices you make and provide a more personalised browsing experience.

Analytics or Performance Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with Town Talk, such as which pages are visited and how visitors navigate the website. We use this information to understand website performance and improve our content.

First-Party and Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are placed directly by Towntalk (“first-party cookies”). Other cookies may be placed by third-party providers that provide services such as analytics, embedded media, advertising, or social-media functionality. Third-party cookies are controlled by the relevant provider and may be subject to that provider’s own privacy and cookie policies.

How Long Cookies Remain

Some cookies are session cookies, which are generally deleted when you close your browser. Others are persistent cookies, which remain on your device for a specified period or until you delete them. The duration depends on the purpose of the cookie and the service that places it.

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Where consent is required, Towntalk will provide an appropriate way for you to manage your cookie preferences. Depending on the cookies used on the website, you may be able to accept all cookies, reject non-essential cookies, or choose which categories of non-essential cookies you wish to allow.

You may also manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality or performance of parts of the website.

Cookies and Personal Data

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes to our website, technologies, services, or applicable legal and regulatory requirements. When we make changes, we will update the “Last Updated” date above.
