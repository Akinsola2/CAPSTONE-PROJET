About TownTalk

TownTalk is a Nigerian-based digital publishing and community platform created to provide a space where blog owners, writers and registered users can publish articles, share ideas, participate in discussions and engage with content across different sections of the website. TownTalk supports a diverse range of voices while maintaining standards designed to promote responsible, accurate, respectful and useful content. This Editorial Policy & Style Guide establishes the standards that apply to content published on TownTalk.

Scope of This Policy

This policy applies to: Articles published by TownTalk; Articles submitted by registered users; Articles submitted by blog owners or contributors; Comments; Replies and discussions; Profiles and biographies; Images, captions and other user-submitted material; Sponsored or promotional content; Editorial features; and Other material published or displayed through TownTalk.

User-Generated Content

TownTalk allows registered users and approved contributors to create and upload content. Users are responsible for the content they submit. By submitting content to TownTalk, a contributor confirms that: They have the necessary rights or permissions to submit the content; The content is not knowingly false or misleading; The content does not unlawfully infringe another person's intellectual-property rights; The content does not unlawfully disclose another person's private or confidential information; The content does not contain malicious software or harmful code; The content complies with applicable Nigerian laws and regulations; and The content complies with the TownTalk Community and Editorial Standards.

Accuracy and Verification

TownTalk aims to publish information that is accurate, relevant and responsibly presented. Contributors should make reasonable efforts to:
Verify factual claims;
Use reliable sources;
Check names, dates, figures and quotations;
Distinguish fact from opinion;
Correct errors when identified; and
Avoid presenting speculation as established fact. Where an article contains allegations concerning an identifiable person or organisation, contributors should take particular care to ensure that the claims are properly supported and fairly presented.

Sources and Attribution

Where information has been obtained from another publication, organisation, research paper, interview, public record or other source, contributors should identify or appropriately credit the source. TownTalk does not permit plagiarism. Direct quotations should be accurately reproduced and appropriately attributed. Where copyrighted material is used, contributors are responsible for ensuring that the use is legally permitted.

Opinions and Commentary

TownTalk may publish opinion, commentary, analysis, reviews and personal perspectives. Opinion should be presented as opinion and should not deliberately be represented as established fact. Contributors should clearly distinguish:
Facts;
Personal opinions;
Analysis;
Predictions;
Allegations; and
Information obtained from third parties.

Corrections

When a significant factual error is identified, TownTalk may:
Correct the article;
Add a correction notice;
Update the relevant information;
Remove inaccurate material; or
Take other appropriate action depending on the circumstances. Where appropriate, significant corrections may include the date on which the correction was made.

Editorial Independence

TownTalk aims to maintain editorial independence. Commercial relationships, advertising, sponsorships or other business relationships should not improperly determine editorial conclusions. Where content has been sponsored, paid for or materially influenced by a commercial relationship, TownTalk may identify it appropriately as sponsored, promotional or commercial content.

Conflicts of Interest

Contributors should disclose material conflicts of interest where they could reasonably affect the credibility or perceived independence of their content. Examples may include:Financial interests;Business relationships;Personal relationships;Ownership interests; Employment relationships; or Other relationships that may materially influence the content.

Comments and Discussions

TownTalk encourages constructive discussion.Comments should:
Relate reasonably to the topic;Remain respectful;
Avoid harassment and threats;
Avoid discriminatory abuse;
Avoid impersonation;
Avoid deliberate misinformation;
Respect other people's privacy.

Prohibited Content

TownTalk may remove or restrict content that includes: Threats of violence;Harassment or targeted abuse;
Hate-based or discriminatory content;
Defamatory or unlawfully harmful material;
Non-consensual disclosure of private information;
Fraudulent or deceptive content;
Malware, malicious code or harmful links;
Spam;
Impersonation;Plagiarised material;
Unlawfully copyrighted material;
Content that sexually exploits or endangers children;
Content that facilitates serious unlawful activity;
Content that violates applicable Nigerian law;
or Other material that TownTalk reasonably determines presents a significant safety, legal or platform-integrity concern.

Privacy of Individuals

Contributors should not publish another person's private information without an appropriate legal basis or permission. This may include:
Private telephone numbers;
Private email addresses;
Home addresses;
Financial information;
Login credentials;
Medical information;
Identification documents;
Private photographs or Other sensitive personal information.
Particular care should be taken when publishing information about children and vulnerable persons.

Images and Multimedia

Contributors are responsible for ensuring that they have the necessary rights or permissions to upload photographs, videos, graphics and other multimedia content. Images should not be manipulated in a manner that deliberately creates a materially misleading impression. Where appropriate, contributors should provide captions or attribution.

AI-Assisted Content

TownTalk permits responsible use of artificial intelligence tools for activities such as brainstorming, editing, research assistance, translation and drafting, provided that the contributor remains responsible for the final content. AI-generated or AI-assisted content must not be used to:
Fabricate sources;Invent quotations;
Deliberately spread false information;
Impersonate people deceptively;Infringe copyright;
Reveal confidential information;
or Circumvent TownTalk's editorial standards.

Sensitive and High-Risk Content

Articles concerning sensitive subjects should be handled responsibly. These may include:
Crime;Accidents and disasters;
Death;
Sexual violence;
Children;
Medical issues;
Mental health;
Allegations of wrongdoing;
Personal trauma;
and Other matters involving significant risk of harm.
Contributors should avoid unnecessary graphic details, sensationalism and disclosure of private information.
