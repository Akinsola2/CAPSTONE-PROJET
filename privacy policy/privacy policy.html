PRIVACY POLICY

Introduction

Welcome to TownTalk. TownTalk is a Nigerian-based blog and digital publishing platform that enables blog owners, writers and registered users to create accounts, publish articles, participate in discussions, comment on content and interact with other users. This Privacy Policy explains how TownTalk collects, uses, stores, shares and protects personal data when you visit or use our website, create an account, publish content, comment, subscribe to communications or otherwise interact with our services. TownTalk is committed to processing personal data fairly, lawfully, transparently and securely in accordance with applicable Nigerian data-protection laws, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDP Act) and applicable directives and regulations. The NDP Act establishes principles governing lawful, fair and transparent processing, purpose limitation, data minimisation, accuracy, storage limitation and security.

Why We Collect Personal Data

TownTalk may process personal data for purposes including:
Creating and managing user accounts;
Authenticating users;
Providing access to TownTalk services;
Publishing user-submitted content;
Displaying author profiles;
Enabling comments and discussions;
Communicating with users;
Responding to enquiries;
Sending newsletters where users have subscribed;
Sending important service communications;
Improving our website and services;
Maintaining platform security;
Detecting and preventing fraud, abuse and malicious activity;
Moderating content;
Enforcing our Terms of Use and policies;
Managing technical operations;
Conducting analytics;
Meeting legal and regulatory obligations;
and Protecting the rights, safety and property of TownTalk, users and others.

Data Minimisation

TownTalk aims to collect personal information that is adequate, relevant and limited to what is reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it is processed. We do not intend to collect unnecessary personal information simply because it is available.

Information Published by Users

Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their account credentials. You should:
Use a strong password;
Avoid sharing your password;
Log out of shared devices;
Notify TownTalk if you suspect unauthorised access; and
Keep your account information accurate. TownTalk will not normally ask you to provide your password through unsolicited email.

Information About Other People

You should not upload another person's personal information unless you have an appropriate lawful basis, permission or other legal justification for doing so. This is particularly important for:
Telephone numbers;
Email addresses;
Home addresses;
Identification information;
Financial information;
Health information;
Private photographs; and
Information concerning children. TownTalk may remove content containing unlawfully disclosed personal information.

Comments and User Interactions

When you comment on an article, your chosen display name, username, profile information and comment may be visible to other users depending on the website's configuration. TownTalk may process information associated with comments for moderation, security, dispute resolution and enforcement of our policies.
